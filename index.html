Questions

Good to know

What if my flight is delayed?

We track every flight live and adjust your pickup time automatically. Delays never cost you extra.

How long will the driver wait?

60 minutes of free waiting from your actual landing time, which is plenty of time for passport control and luggage.

How do I pay?

Pay the driver by card or cash, or ask for a payment link when you book on WhatsApp.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before pickup.
